# Athenks
Athenks — Private B2B Wholesale Shirt Catalog

Vibe Coders Hackathon Edition 3 · "Built for One" Track
Live App: https://athenks.onrender.com

A mobile-first B2B wholesale shirt catalog and ordering app built for Chail Singh, owner of Athenks—a wholesale shirt manufacturer in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Why It Exists

Every week, new shirt samples arrive from the factory. Because every wholesale buyer has a different negotiated rate, staff currently spend hours editing design numbers and private prices onto photos, forwarding them separately on WhatsApp, and decoding messy chat orders over phone calls.

Athenks replaces that loop: upload a photo once, tie private rates to each party's 15-digit GST number, and send clean orders straight to staff for billing.

Core Features

GSTIN-Linked Private Pricing: Zero passwords or OTPs. Buyers enter their Company Name and 15-digit GSTIN to unlock only their own negotiated rates.

Real Wholesale Math: Minimum order 1 Set = 9 pieces (sizes 28 to 36); bulk 1 Carton = 30 Sets (270 pieces).

Auto Godown Stock: Confirmed orders automatically deduct exact pieces and mark designs Out of stock below 9 pieces.

Private Staff Panel (/admin): Manage per-GSTIN custom prices (Parties List), upload designs with auto-compression & restock (Inventory), and view ready-to-bill orders (Orders).

Intentional Restraint: Deliberately zero payment gateways, shipping trackers, bill generators, or retail clutter—built to fit how Indian wholesale actually runs.
